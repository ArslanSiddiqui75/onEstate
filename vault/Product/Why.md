# Why

Real estate teams run fragmented stacks: separate CRM, portals, compliance, website, social, and billing. **0neStreet** is the name in both regions. The UK and US setups share one multi-tenant product with role-aware modules and market-specific config.

One codebase; brand chosen at deploy via `NEXT_PUBLIC_BRAND`.

Surfaces: marketing `/`, workspace `/app`, platform `/admin`, public sites `/site/[slug]`.
